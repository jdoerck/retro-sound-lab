Scanline Workbench2-channel 5-bit divider sound · modeled on the Atari 2600 TIA (1977)

Play a key to start the chip.

The bar under each key shows how close the chip can get: green within 15 cents, amber within 35, red further off. Z–M and Q–I on a keyboard.

The audio clock runs twice per scanline (about 31.4 kHz on NTSC). Each mode's waveform is rebuilt from public descriptions of the chip's polynomial counters and dividers, so the character is right but individual noise patterns are not bit-exact.
