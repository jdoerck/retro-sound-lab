CSG WorkbenchComplex sound generator · modeled on the TI SN76477 (1978)

Press and hold Gate to start the chip.

Hold a key to gate the chip with the oscillator transposed. Keys Z–M and Q–I on a keyboard; Space holds the gate.

Export renders the patch with the gate held for "Hold" seconds, then released, for "Length" in total. The zip holds a 44.1 kHz WAV and the patch. Frequencies and times use simplified RC formulas, so they're close to the chip's behavior, not bench-measured.
