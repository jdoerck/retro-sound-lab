Wavetable Workbench32-oscillator 8-bit wavetable · modeled on the Ensoniq 5503 DOC (mid-1980s)

Each note takes an oscillator pair. Z–M and Q–I on a keyboard.

U2Wave memory

Single-cycle tables can be drawn on. Loaded audio is resampled to 26.3 kHz, cut to the chosen length and stored as 8-bit, stopping at its end marker like the chip's one-shot samples.

Export plays the last notes you held (or middle C). Patches store settings and generated wave recipes; loaded audio stays in this browser's wave memory and is not part of the patch. Frequency registers and update rates follow the chip's design; the filter stage stands in for the analog filters boards placed after the chip.
