U1Pattern editor

Instrument

Tap a cell, then a step pad to enter a note. On a keyboard, the Z, A and Q rows play the pads in order. Gold-edged pads start a new period. Arrows move, Delete clears, the backquote key enters a note off. Changing instrument or effect also applies it to the selected note.

U2Order list

Each entry calls a pattern, optionally transposed by whole steps of the current tuning. A repeated pattern costs 3 bytes per channel, not a second copy of its notes. The noise channel ignores transposition.

U3Timing

Frame rate

The driver runs once per video frame. Durations are stored in rows and multiplied by this speed, so changing it rescales every note.

U4Tuning

Master clock

Every pitch is the master clock divided by a whole number, then stepped through the 24-bit register. A slow clock leaves few divisors to choose from, so any scale bends toward the clock's own subharmonic series. Register patterns that repeat early sound a twelfth or two octaves up, intervals the piano keyboard never lines up with.
