DAC-8 WorkbenchCPU-driven 8-bit DAC synthesis · after the 1980–81 Williams sound board

Keys Z–M and Q–U play two octaves (C4 is the patch as set). Space plays the patch. Tap the overview to move the zoom window.

Export gives a zip with two WAVs: the raw 8-bit stream at the DAC rate, and a 44.1 kHz 16-bit version through the output filter, ready for a sampler.
