The 24 bits loop out of the register one at a time. The shortest repeating unit sets the pitch: a pattern that repeats every 8 bits plays three times higher than one that only repeats after 24. With "tune to period" on, the keyboard compensates so every pattern stays in tune.

Records up to 30 seconds of what you play, then offers it as a WAV.

The register, shift clock, noise and 4-bit volume follow the design of the console's sound hardware. Playable pitch, scanning and extra voices are additions for musical use.
