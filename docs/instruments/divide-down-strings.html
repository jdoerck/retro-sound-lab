Divide-Down StringsTop-octave generator, divider chains and ensemble · a 1970s string machine on one board

Fully polyphonic: every key has its own keyer, fed from the shared generator. Latch to hold chords while you adjust. Z–M and Q–I on a keyboard.

Records up to 30 seconds in stereo, then offers it as a WAV.

Twelve master oscillators are divided down from one clock and halved again for every octave, so every note of a pitch class is phase-locked, and detuning the clock detunes the whole instrument at once. The ensemble uses three modulated delay lines. This is an original model of the technique, not of a particular instrument.
