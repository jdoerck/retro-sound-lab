Night Circuit DrumsDiscrete analog percussion · in the style of late-1970s and early-1980s drum machines

U9Output stage

One-shots export dry, each in a normal and an accented version, ready for a sampler. The loop exports through the output stage. Timings follow the RC rule of thumb (1 / 2πRC for resonators, R × C for decays). The circuits are original designs in the era's style, not copies of any machine.
