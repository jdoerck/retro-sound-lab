Beeper WorkbenchOne speaker pin, every trick · 1-bit music in the style of early home computers

Play a key to start the loop.

Hold several keys for chords. Latch keeps them sounding so you can tweak with both hands free. Z–M and Q–I on a keyboard.

Records up to 30 seconds of what you play, then offers it as a WAV.

Every sound here leaves through a single on/off pin, as on machines whose only sound hardware was a speaker toggled by the CPU. The techniques are the ones the 1-bit music scene uses; this engine is an original implementation of them, not a copy of any particular routine.
