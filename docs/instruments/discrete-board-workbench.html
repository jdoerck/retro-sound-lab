Discrete Board WorkbenchTransistor-and-RC game sound circuits · in the style of late-1970s vector arcade boards

Press a control to power the board.

Heartbeat and saucer latch on and off; thrust sounds while held. Keys are shown on each control.

Records up to 30 seconds of whatever you play, then offers it as a WAV.

Each circuit's timing follows the usual RC rule of thumb (about 0.7 × R × C for oscillator periods, R × C for decays), so part swaps change sounds the way they would on a bench. These are original circuits written for this tool, not a copy of any game's schematic.
